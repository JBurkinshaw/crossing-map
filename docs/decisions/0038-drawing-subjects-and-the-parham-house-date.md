# 0038. Drawings' subjects that give nothing away, and the Parham house dated 2-05

Status: accepted (2026-11-16). Owner's decisions after a review of the site's text. Extends 0036.
Phase: P1

## Context

Decision 0036 renamed the stops and drawing captions that told an episode's outcome, but left each drawing's `stands_for`, the line in the published data that says what the drawing stands for. Sixteen of them still told the event: "The river camp near San José where Boyd is shot", "The truck that carries the wounded Boyd away", "Boyd's stolen horse found again in Bacerac", "Where Billy finds the she-wolf in his trap". The site does not show the field, but a drawing is published from its own episode, so a data user reading up to that point met the outcome.

The Parham house stop was dated 2-04, the episode in which Billy comes home, finds the house empty, and hears from the Sanders what happened. A reader who set their progress to 2-04 at its start saw the house, and the SK Bar stop after it, before reading the episode.

## Decision

- **`stands_for` follows 0036.** A drawing's `stands_for` names the place, the ride or the thing, not who dies, is hurt, is lost, is found, or how an attempt ends. The 16 that did are rewritten, for example "The river camp near San José (J4)", "The farmworkers' truck on the dirt road in open country (J4, J6)", "Keno at Bacerac (2-07)", "The trap site on Cloverdale Creek (1-08)". The event stays in the drawing's note and reasoning.
- **The Parham house shows from 2-05.** The stop, its drawing and the open question about where the house stood are dated 2-05. The SK Bar stop comes after the house on J3, and its reasoning says what the Sanders tell Billy, so it is dated 2-05 too; so are the three legs that join the pass, the house, the SK Bar and Animas. Each reasoning still cites 2-04 as the episode where it happens and says why the stop waits a step. The 2-05 summary ("Billy returns to the family house") already agrees.

## Alternatives

- **Leave `stands_for` as it is, since the site does not show it.** The data is published, and its users read it by episode as the site does.
- **Date only the house to 2-05.** The SK Bar stop and the leg into it would then show at 2-04 without the stop they lead from.

## Consequences

A reader in 2-04 sees J3 end at the pass in the Guadalupes, and the house, the SK Bar and the legs between them appear from 2-05, one episode later than the book reaches them. The Echols cabin drawing stands at the SK Bar's point from 1-02; the SK Bar's own pin appears only from 2-05.
