# 0081. A large title at the scale of the drawings

Status: accepted (2026-11-20)
Phase: P1

## Context

The owner's model for the map's look, the Trail of Blood Meridian map, carries its title in large red capitals across the top. The site's only title on the map was the small cartouche in the corner. The owner asked for a large title at the same scale as the large drawings (decisions 0076 and 0079).

## Decision

- **"The Crossing" across the top of the story**, centred on 108.6 W, 35.75 N, above Winslow and Albuquerque, where no stop or route line lies (the story's northern edge is 35.08 N).
- **Type, not a drawing:** the map's IM Fell English small capitals, in J3's oxblood with a thin ink edge, after the Blood Meridian map's red title. Type stays sharp at every size and needs no image.
- **The same scale as the drawings.** Its size doubles with each zoom step, as the land does: 42 px at zoom 5, about 470 px across at the whole-story view (5.6). It fades with the drawings between zoom 7 and 8, draws under every name and line, and blocks no label. Like the drawings, it hides over satellite and in the Oblique view.

## Alternatives

- **A drawn title** in the drawings' pen. It would need its own image and look rougher than the map's type.
- **A fixed-size title.** It would not read as part of the map's engraving, and at zoom 8 and closer it would cover the story.

## Consequences

The title shows wherever the large drawings do. The corner cartouche stays, for the closer zooms where the title has faded.
