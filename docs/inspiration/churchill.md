# Carl Churchill's "Variety in hachure" references

Oblique and block-diagram maps from Carl Churchill's post [Variety in hachure](https://churchillgeo.com/variety-in-hachure/) (8 June 2025), studied for the Oblique view's looks (`review/experiments/raised-styles/`). The images belong to their holders, so none of them goes in this repository. Copies of the post's previews, fetched on 16 November 2026, are in `private/inspiration/churchill/variety-in-hachure/`, which is git-ignored.

| Map | Date | What the lab takes from it | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tau Rho Alpha et al., Monterey Canyon, *Atlas of Oblique Maps* (USGS I-1799), p. 81 | 1988 | Long fall-line strokes, dense in shade and sparse in light; level dashes on the flats | [USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/i1799) |
| Frank R. Holmes, *Estado da Guanabara, Relêvo e Hidrografia* | 1965 | Fans of tapered strokes from each crest, firm crest outlines, stippled lowlands (Percy Lau's manner) | [IBGE, Revista Brasileira de Geografia 27(4)](https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/periodicos/115/rbg_1965_v27_n4.pdf) |
| American Geographical Society, block diagram of the Trentino | 1918 | Pencil strokes heavy at the top and fading downhill; blue water and red roads on tan paper; the cut block | [Leventhal Map Center](https://collections.leventhalmap.org/search/commonwealth:ht2509105) |
| Charles H. Owens, *The Panama Canal*, Los Angeles Times | 1928 | The "comic-book" strokes: shade as solid black flames, lit faces left blank | [UWM Libraries, AGS Library](https://collections.lib.uwm.edu/digital/collection/agdm/id/32853) |
| U.S. Exploring Expedition, *Map of Upolu Island, Samoa* | 1839 | Hachures with land cover drawn in profile at an exaggerated size; villages as small blocks | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Upolu_map,_US_Ex._Ex.,_1839.jpg) |

The Oblique view follows three of them, Alpha, Holmes and Owens (decision 0040).

The post also points to Daniel Huffman's 3D hachures, [A new take on an old style](https://somethingaboutmaps.wordpress.com/2024/10/29/a-new-take-on-an-old-style/) (2024), the closest modern example of strict hachures in perspective.
