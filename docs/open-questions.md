# Open questions

Places and readings that the evidence so far does not settle. Each entry says what is shown now and what would settle it. A place listed here says so on its card on the site, with a link to suggest a correction.

The reasoning for each pick is in `pipeline/seeds/parts2-4-place-overrides.csv` and `pipeline/seeds/part1-place-overrides.csv`. The site shows a short form of each question, and the stage 7 judgement calls, from `data/open-questions.json`: on the cards of the features concerned and on its Open questions page. Keep the two in step.

## Places shown, with a doubt

| Sierra de la Madera | The Sky Island range near Cumpas (GeoNames 9028461) | Bell (2000) and Joe Hazelton's map take the range on the upper Bavispe, 81 km north-east, but from there the wolf would not cross the Bavispe as the text has her do | A source that names the wolf's range, or a reading that puts her west of the river |
| Soto Máynez | One point with Santa Ana de Babícora | INEGI gives Soto Máynez as Santa Ana de Babícora's official name, but in 3-12 girls walk toward Soto Maynez after the riders pass back through Santa Ana, as if it were another town | A 1940s map or account that shows both names, or a reader who knows the area |
| Nahuerichic | Nahuerachi, 7 km south-south-west of Madera (GeoNames 13416932) | The name differs by one vowel, and no source yet ties the Babícora division to this place. Bell 2000 independently places a Nahuerachic about 65 miles from San Buenaventura; ours is 63 miles, but he too found nothing tying it to the estate | A history of the Babícora estate's divisions |
| La Boquilla | La Boquilla in Gómez Farías municipio, by the Babícora lake | The text also calls it Boquilla y Anexas, which INEGI places in Guerrero municipio, 135 km from Las Varas | Already decided for the lake town (stage 4 review); listed because the name is shared |
| San José | San José de Ermita, 48 km north-north-west of La Boquilla | A common name; this one fits the brothers' ride north up a river, but nothing in the text names its municipio | A reader who knows the upper Casas Grandes drainage |
| Sierra del Nido | The range east-south-east of San Buenaventura | The text sees it to the south | The text's direction may be loose; no change planned |
| Namiquipa | The town | The text sends Billy west from Santa Ana to reach it; the real town is 25 km north-north-east | The text's own geography; no change planned |
