# Stop data to check, found while writing the synopses

The Parts II to IV synopses were drafted from the text of each stop's episode, then checked against the text by a second reader (decision 0051). Both passes compared each stop's row in `pipeline/seeds/stops.csv` with the book. The synopses already follow the text. These rows' other columns do not, and are left for the owner.

## Placement reasoning that shows too early

The site hides a stop's reasoning while it cites a later episode by number. These two don't cite one, so they show from the stop's own episode.

- `j3-lordsburg` (2-05): says Billy tells the sheriff and learns Boyd is on the east side of town. Both happen in 2-06 and 2-07. In 2-05 he only rides in at noon.
- `j3-websters` (2-06): says Boyd is lodging with the Websters. The book first puts Boyd there at the start of 2-07. In 2-06 the Websters' is named once, as where some household goods went. Either cite 2-07 in the reasoning or date the stop 2-07.

## Placement reasoning that says more, or other, than the book

- `j3-sk-bar`: says Sanders and Leona tell Billy what happened. The book never shows the telling, only its effect.
- `j4-casas-grandes`: puts the plank-bridge crossing at Casas Grandes. The book puts it in Colonia Juárez, after the orchards.
- `j4-escarpment-rim`: says they reach the rim the morning after Bacerac. It takes several days: a camp by a hill farm, a night in a burn, then two days through the high country.
- `j5-san-diego`: says Boyd asks Billy to meet “the girl” at Santa Ana de Babícora. Boyd asks him to go to Namiquipa, and Billy comes on her by chance on the road west of Santa Ana.
- `j6-san-lorenzo`: says “the girl” was carried off. The book says she was taken away.
- `j7-attack`: cites 4-07 for the gypsies doctoring Niño, which is all in 4-06. The citation hides the reasoning from readers in 4-06 for no reason.
- `j7-cemetery`: says Billy buries Boyd there. The book shows him digging until the sheriff stops him, and never says the burial is finished.
- `j7-deming`: says the sergeant at the armory finds the heart murmur. A doctor in town finds it, and the sergeant does the paperwork. It also leaves out the ten days at the stable before El Paso.
- `j7-magdalena`: "Nine months on" is nine more months with the same outfit. Minor.

## Moments with no stop of their own

- 2-06: the sheriff's interview in Lordsburg, the episode's main scene. A Lordsburg stop dated 2-06 would hold it.
- 2-08: the second Bacerac scene (the old man's map, the directions east). `j4-bacerac` is dated 2-07, so its synopsis stops there.
- 3-07: the one-armed man's fall at La Boquilla. `j4-boquilla` is dated 3-06, though its reasoning describes the fall and cites 3-07.
- 4-05: the wake at Namiquipa. `j7-namiquipa` is dated 4-04, and its reasoning describes the wake and cites 4-05.
